Five ways to make gift giving worse

Landing page directions for the Shitebox store, built from the brand book and the decisions of 27 to 30 October. Same content and prices in every version so only the design changes. Open each one full size; they all work at phone width.

01

Pink Shout

The brand book, literally. Obnoxious pink, acid yellow signage, stickers that break the grid, a marquee of brand lines. Loud and unmistakable.

Best if: the name stays front and centre and the site is mostly reached from TikTok.

02

Black Market

Near-black premium editorial with one neon accent per section and the magnetic-box reveal as the hero object. A deliberately bad gift, a seriously good brand.

Best if: Scott's magnetic box wins and the brand wants to feel considered rather than cheap.
Open full page
03

Unlucky Dip

The parent-brand storefront. Purple and lime masthead that is safe for ads and branded tape; Shitebox is the first product line with tiles for Corporate, Birthday, Merch and Festive.

Best if: the ad-safe parent brand goes ahead and sub-brands follow.
Open full page
04

Rave Flyer

1990s rave poster meets discount flyer. Acid yellow base, visible 4px grid, blocks that touch, condensed poster type, stickers at angles. Nothing is centred.

Best if: the brand wants maximum character and a look nobody else in gifting has.
Open full page
05

Clean Shop

A conventional premium store layout: white space, product cards, reviews, trust strip. Pink is the only accent and the voice does the rest.

Best if: conversion and trust matter more than attitude, or as the Shopify theme base to customise.
