How it works

Three steps to a worse Christmas

Pick a size

Small, big or absolute. That is the only decision you make, and we would rather you didn't overthink it.

We fill it

Four kinds of thing go in every box: one branded Experience item, mundane objects, novelty rubbish and a card asking for a reaction video.

They open it

On camera, ideally. The card inside has a box number. Type it in to see what each item is and why it exists.
