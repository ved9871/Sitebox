Frequently asked questions. Unfortunately.

What is inside?

Stuff. Mostly. Some mundane shite and some novelty shite, four, six or nine items depending on the box. Random, low-value goods. You are paying for the laugh, and we say so at checkout.

Is any of it second-hand?

Yes. Around 40% of our stock is rescued from charity shops in Lincoln. Nothing that is a hygiene risk is ever second-hand. Buying from us supports those charities, which is more than you'd planned to do today.

I don't know what to get someone.

Then stop. That's the whole idea. You pick a size, we take the decision, and if it's rubbish you tell them to blame us. We're used to it.

Can I return it?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to cancel for any reason, and faulty items are sorted properly and politely. The 500-word essay on our returns page is a joke. The form underneath it is not.

Can I have a discount?

You have already got questionable taste. Do not push it. (There is a code inside the lid.)
