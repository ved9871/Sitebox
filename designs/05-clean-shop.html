How it works

Three steps, one regret

1

Pick a size

Small, big or absolute. That is the only decision you make.

2

One branded Experience item, mundane objects, novelty rubbish and a card asking for a reaction video. The mix is random; the count is not.

What's inside

The gloriously ordinary

Everyday objects are funny precisely because they are so unremarkable.

Experience

Things only we make: a Certificate of Disappointment, an envelope containing the word No, a scratchcard that only says Unlucky.

Mundane

A toothbrush, a sponge, a wooden spoon, clothes pegs, a shoehorn, a deeply boring coaster.

Novelty

A kazoo, a harmonica, a badge that says FFS, a photo frame with the stock image still inside.

Marketing

A card inviting the recipient to film their reaction. The reaction is the product.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions. Unfortunately.

What is inside?

Stuff. Mostly. Four, six or nine items drawn from the four categories above. Contents are random, low-value novelty and household goods. You are paying for the laugh and we say so before you pay.

Is it worth it?

Define worth.

Can I return it?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to cancel for any reason, and faulty items are replaced or refunded properly. The essay on the returns page is a joke; the form under it is not.

Can I have a discount?

You have already got questionable taste. Do not push it. There is a code inside the lid.

Do you recommend it?

We barely recommend ourselves.
