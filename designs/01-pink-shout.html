Stuff. Mostly.

Every box is built from four kinds of thing. The mix is random. The disappointment is not.

Experience

Branded things only we make. An envelope containing the word No. A scratchcard that says Unlucky. A pen with nothing to say.

Mundane

A toothbrush. A sink plug. A tea towel. Everyday objects are funny precisely because they are so unremarkable.

Novelty

A kazoo. A harmonica. A badge that says FFS. Things people pick up and mess around with.

Marketing

A card that asks for the one thing we actually want: their face, on video, at the moment of realisation.

Frequently asked questions. Unfortunately.

What is inside?

Stuff. Mostly. Four, six or nine items depending on the box, drawn from the four categories above. The contents are random, low-value novelty and household goods. You are paying for the laugh, and we say so at checkout.

Is it worth it?

Define worth.

Can I return it?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to cancel for any reason under the Consumer Contracts Regulations, and faulty items are sorted properly and politely. The 500-word essay on our returns page is a joke. The form underneath it is not.

Can I have a discount?

You have already got questionable taste. Do not push it. (There is a code inside the lid.)

Do you recommend it?

We barely recommend ourselves.
