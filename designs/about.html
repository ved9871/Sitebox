About us. Reluctantly.

We're shy.

Two blokes from Lincolnshire who got fed up choosing presents and decided nobody else should have to either. We won't tell you our names. We'll barely show you our faces.

SDoes the shopping
VDoes the spreadsheets

Why we take the decision off you

Buying a present is a gamble you keep losing. We've simply removed the gamble by removing the choice.

You pick a box size. That's your whole job. We fill it with some mundane shite and some novelty shite, you send it, and whoever opens it has nobody to blame but us. We're fine with that. We've been blamed for worse.

S finds the stuff. V counts the stuff. Between us we have the taste of a wet weekend in Skegness, and we've turned that into a product.

Every box is different. Every box is a surprise. Every box has a card at the bottom that says: that's it, stop looking.

40%

Around 40% of the shite is recycled

A lot of what we sell is rescued from charity shops across Lincoln, which means buying from us supports those charities too. We go round them with a budget and no taste, and we film it. Some items will be second-hand. Nothing that is a hygiene risk is ever second-hand.
