What goes inside

The gloriously ordinary

Four kinds of thing in every box. The mix is random; the categories are not. Everyday objects are funny precisely because they are so unremarkable.

E

Experience

Things only we make. A Certificate of Disappointment. An envelope containing the word No. A scratchcard that only says Unlucky. One in every box.

M

Mundane

A toothbrush, a washing-up sponge, a wooden spoon, clothes pegs, a shoehorn, a deeply boring coaster.

N

Novelty

A kazoo, a harmonica, a badge that says FFS, a photo frame with the stock image still inside.

?

Marketing

A card inviting the recipient to film their reaction. The reaction, not the product, is what travels.
